SERVER #13

Freak Fortress 2

01
No camping or delaying as the Boss

If you are selected as the Boss, actively play the round and engage the RED team instead of intentionally delaying it.

Don't want to be selected? Use !ff2toggle.
02
No teaming

Play for your own side. RED should damage the Boss and help the team, while the Boss should actively fight RED.

03
Spectate when AFK

If you need to go away from the game, join Spectator instead of remaining spawned in the round.

AFK players can increase the Boss's health and leave more work for active teammates.
04
Ghosting exception for prolonged delaying

Normal ghosting rules still apply. An exception may be made when revealing a player who has been camping for an extended period helps move an otherwise stalled round along.

05
RED camping depends on the class

Camping rules for RED are class-dependent. Check the official Panda rules thread for the exact class-specific restrictions and exceptions.
